# Content map and Finnish adaptation rules

Status: content plan for approval before implementation. No Lithuanian business text should be copied into production.

## 1. Content ownership model

| Content domain | Authoritative owner/source | Finnish treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Product identity and technical facts | Manufacturer product master / current technical documents | Preserve meaning, translate professionally, record source/version/SKU |
| Manufacturer story/process | Manufacturer-approved corporate source | Short factual brand section; do not present it as the Finnish company story |
| Finnish distributor identity | `[OUR COMPANY NAME]` | Original Finnish copy with legal facts and authorized-distributor wording |
| Finnish price, stock, lead time, delivery, samples | Finnish distributor systems/policies | Publish only current supplied values; otherwise “Pyydä tarjous” |
| Installation service | Finnish service owner + manufacturer instructions | Original Finnish service scope, with source-approved technical requirements |
| Claims/certifications/warranty | Applicable manufacturer document and Finnish review | Claim registry; publish only verified applicable wording |
| Legal/privacy/cookies | Finnish legal/business owner | New Finnish documents based on actual tools/processes |
| Gallery imagery | Manufacturer/distributor rights records | Caption and relate to exact products only when known |
| SEO/editorial Finnish | Finnish content owner | Natural intent-led copy without changing technical facts |

## 3. Catalog and product content map

### Category page

- Finnish category name and short factual introduction.
- Child categories/collections in manufacturer-approved order.
- Optional buying guidance: use, appearance, construction, installation compatibility.
- Product cards from active Finnish assortment only.
- Relevant installation and care guidance.
- Quote/sample CTA.
- SEO title/description and canonical.

### Product page

Required when source data exists:

- Product name, manufacturer/brand, SKU, category and collection.
- Short Finnish description and complete reviewed description.
- Color, surface, finish.
- Length, width, thickness and tolerances with units.
- Package size/coverage/quantity and weight where applicable.
- Installation method and compatible installation products.
- Maintenance guidance and compatible care products.
- Ordered technical specifications.
- Technical documents with date/version/language and applicable SKU.
- Approved images, alt text, captions and rights metadata.
- Explicit related/accessory/sample relations.

Optional, Finnish-owned:

- Price and pricing basis (`€/m²`, `€/kpl`, `€/pakkaus`, etc.).
- Availability and last update.
- Lead time.
- Area/package calculator.
- Finnish delivery note.

Never infer a missing specification from a similar product. Never parse dimensions or prices from a legacy slug as authoritative data. Missing commercial data renders a quote CTA.

### Product naming pattern

Use a consistent readable sequence rather than copying price/dimension-packed legacy slugs:

`[Product type] [collection/model] – [color], [finish]`

Dimensions belong in the key facts and may appear in the title only when they distinguish an otherwise ambiguous panel/accessory. URLs should be stable, lower-case, concise, and independent of price.

Required sections:

3. Manufacturer/brand introduction as a separate entity.

## 8. Finnish terminology and search intent

| Intent | Preferred Finnish usage | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| bamboo flooring | `bambulattia`, plural `bambulattiat` | Broad primary term |
| bamboo parquet | `bambuparketti` / `bambuparkettilattia` | Use only for products that qualify as parquet in construction/trade usage |
| bamboo decking | `bambuterassi`, `bambuterassilauta`, plural `bambuterassilaudat` | Distinguish board/product from completed terrace |
| flooring installation | `lattian asennus`, `bambulattian asennus` | Service wording must match actual scope |
| durable flooring | `kulutusta kestävä lattia`, `pitkäikäinen lattia` | Requires product evidence; avoid universal claim |
| sustainable flooring | Prefer precise evidenced wording: material origin, renewability, lifecycle or certification | `kestävä` is ambiguous; never use generic green claims without proof |
| underfloor heating | `lattialämmitykseen soveltuva bambulattia` | Only on verified compatible products and with conditions |
| skirting board | `jalkalista` | Confirm profiles/dimensions |
| stair nose/element | `porrasnokka`, `porrasosa` | Select term per actual product |
| bamboo panel | `bambulevy`, `bambukalustelevy` | Distinguish construction/use |
| surface/finish | `pinta`, `pintakäsittely`, `viimeistely` | Use consistent spec vocabulary |
| floating/glued installation | `uiva asennus`, `liima-asennus` | Only where manufacturer permits |

SEO copy should answer customer intent first. Do not repeat keyword variants unnaturally or turn technical pages into sales claims.

## 9. Claims and source workflow

Each claim moves through:

```text
source document received
→ applicable product/SKU and market identified
→ factual Finnish translation
→ technical/business approval
→ publication with source/version metadata
→ scheduled review or withdrawal when superseded
```

Claim types requiring this workflow include environmental/CO₂/renewability statements, emissions, fire class, biological resistance, hardness comparisons, thermal values, underfloor-heating limits, certifications, warranties, durability/lifespan and installation conditions.

## 11. Pre-publication content checklist

- Distributor and manufacturer are visually and legally distinct.
- Finnish assortment owner confirms every published product/category.
- Every number, technical condition, certification, warranty and environmental statement has an applicable source.
- Temperature and other cross-page values are reconciled.
- Prices, VAT basis, stock, lead times, sample and delivery policies are current or hidden.
- Installation scope, area, preparation, exclusions and quote handling are approved.
- Image reuse rights, credits, product relations and alt text are recorded.
- Forms have real destinations, privacy copy and tested success/error paths.
- Finnish copy is human-reviewed; `bambuparketti` and sustainability language are used precisely.
- SEO metadata is unique and structured data contains no invented offers/reviews.
