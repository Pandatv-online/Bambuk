About bamboo

Bamboo is a perennial, naturally growing, evergreen grass. It is one of the most resistant and fast-growing plants - the bamboo root system quickly releases new shoots, so the plants do not need to be replanted after cutting. To get the full range of wood raw materials, bamboo is enough to grow for 3-5 years.
Because natural bamboo flooring waste is used to produce treated bamboo flooring, no further waste is generated. This is the floor covering that generates the lowest amount of waste.
In the production of pressed bamboo floor, the smallest amount of adhesives is used, which is only 5% of the total weight of the table, preventing not only the layering of the material, but also various possible mechanical damage to the floor.
El. catalogue

In the current floor market, the bamboo flooring is considered one of the best choices for floor coverings due to extremely high resistance, stability and thermal conductivity.
Because of its strength, the product is ideal for both practical people who want to lay floor cover and just "forget it" and for those who want to have an exclusive design floor, fitting in any style or interior.

Bamboo flooring features

Durability
The durability of treated bamboo flooring is unmatched, often due to this feature, the treated bamboo flooring becomes the best choice for large areas of motion.

Beyond technology
Because natural bamboo flooring waste is used to produce treated bamboo flooring, no further waste is generated. This is the floor covering that generates the lowest amount of waste.

Heat conductivity
Extremely good thermal conductivity due to the properties of highly dense material. The thermal resistance of a 14 mm thick bamboo panel floorboard - 0,053m2K / W

Resistance to moisture
Due to the respective processing methods, the treated bamboo fiber floor is much more resistant to moisture than traditional bamboo flooring, which greatly prolongs the life of the floor covering.
