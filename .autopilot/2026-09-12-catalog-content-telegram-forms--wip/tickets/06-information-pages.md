## Критерии приёмки

- [ ] Hub and six documented routes render from structured Finnish content records
- [ ] Every technical statement retains internal source and applicability; unsupported claims/numbers are omitted
- [ ] Installation content clearly says project specifics are confirmed in quotation; no invented service area/method/price
- [ ] Related product/category links are present only where the relation is explicit
- [ ] Each page has one H1, unique metadata, semantic sections and no filler/duplicate copy
- [ ] Tests cover route registry, source presence and forbidden-claim scan
